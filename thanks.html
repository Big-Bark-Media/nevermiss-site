Here's what happens next. You don't need to do anything until we're in touch.

  1. We register your number

    We submit your business details for the UK number checks that Ofcom requires. This can take a few working days. We'll tell you if anything needs correcting.

  2. We book your set-up call

    We'll be in touch within one working day to book a 20-minute call. Together we'll check your texts and greeting, and set up your alerts and dashboard login.

  3. You switch on your divert

    On the call we'll give you a short code to dial on your mobile, matched to your network. It sends calls you don't answer to Never Miss a Lead instead of your voicemail. For example: **61* then your new number, then #.

    Your phone still rings first, as normal. You can switch the divert off any time with ##61#.

  4. We test it together

    We ring you, you don't answer, and we check the text arrives and your alert comes through. Then you're live.

Questions in the meantime? Message us, or book your set-up call now if you'd like to pick a time.
