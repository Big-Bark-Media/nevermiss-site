Here's what happens next. You don't need to do anything until we email you.

  1. We register your number

    We submit your business details for the UK number checks that Ofcom requires. This can take a few working days. We'll tell you if anything needs correcting.

    If you're a sole trader or partnership, we'll also send you a secure link to upload photo ID for the check. Please don't send ID by email or WhatsApp.

  2. We set it up behind the scenes

    We write your texts and AI business info from your form, set up your alerts and, if you chose it, your WhatsApp Business profile. If we host your website, we add the button to it for you.

  3. You get your go-live email

    When your number's ready, we email you a link to your dashboard and a set of short setup videos (nevermiss.bigbark.media/setup). Sign in, add the dashboard to your Home Screen and turn on phone notifications.

  4. You switch on your divert

    The email includes a short code to dial on your mobile, matched to your network, and the "Divert your missed calls" video shows you how. It sends calls you don't answer to Never Miss a Lead instead of your voicemail. For example: **61* then your new number, then #.

    Your phone still rings first, as normal. You can switch the divert off any time with ##61#.

  5. You test it with a missed call

    Ask someone to ring your mobile and don't answer. Check the text reaches them and your alert comes through, then check your business info in the dashboard. Your 7-day free trial starts when your number goes live.
