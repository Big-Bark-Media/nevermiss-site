Getting started

All plans

Get alerts on your phone

All plans

  1. Go to my.bigbark.media, enter your email and type the 6-digit code we send you. No password.
  2. iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”. (iOS 16.4 or later.)
  3. Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”), then “Install”.
  4. Open Never Miss a Lead from the new icon on your Home Screen.
  5. Go to Settings, tap “Turn on notifications on this phone”, then “Allow”. Tap “Send a test notification” to check.

Divert your missed calls

All plans. Swap NUMBER for your Never Miss a Lead number (it's in your go-live email and in Settings, Divert codes).

  1. When you don't answer: dial **61*NUMBER# and press call.
  2. When you're busy or tap decline: **67*NUMBER#
  3. When your phone is off or has no signal: **62*NUMBER#
  4. On iPhone, don't use Settings, Phone, Call Forwarding: that sends every call away.
  5. Test it: from another phone, ring your mobile and let it ring out. You should get an alert, and the other phone should get your text.
  6. Check a divert: *#61#. Switch every divert off: ##002#. Vodafone pay as you go can't divert: message us and we'll sort another way.

Replying to enquiries

All plans. The AI parts are for Smart Text and AI Receptionist.

  1. Your inbox shows who's waiting for a reply and where they came from.
  2. Open a conversation, type your reply and send. It goes from your Never Miss a Lead number, in your business's name.
  3. The AI replies first, says it's automated, uses only your info and hands bites, aggression and complaints to you.
  4. As soon as you reply or tap call, the AI goes quiet in that conversation. You can also tap “Pause AI”.
  5. Text alerts? Reply to the alert starting with the conversation's code, like #4821 your message.

WhatsApp: your website button and ads

Smart Text and AI Receptionist

  1. Your “WhatsApp us” website button opens a WhatsApp chat with your business. We install it on sites we host; anyone else, email us for the code.
  2. Facebook Page settings, Linked accounts, WhatsApp: enter your Never Miss a Lead number and tap “Send code”.
  3. The code arrives in your Never Miss a Lead inbox. Type it into Facebook.
  4. In Ads Manager choose Messaging apps, then WhatsApp. For Google ads, send people to your website: the button tags them as from Google.
  5. Every chat shows which ad it came from, and Results adds them up each month.

Test your AI receptionist

AI Receptionist

  1. In Settings, check your prices, classes and area. The AI uses only what's written there.
  2. From another phone, ring your mobile and let it ring out. Your AI receptionist answers.
  3. Ask it something, give a name and a dog's name, then hang up.
  4. Within a minute or two you get a notification and a written summary in your inbox.
  5. 150 AI minutes are included each month. Calls you answer yourself never use them.
