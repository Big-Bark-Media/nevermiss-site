Privacy notice

Last updated 18 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This notice explains how Big Bark Media uses personal information in running Never Miss a Lead, our missed-call text-back, AI text reply and AI receptionist service. It covers people who visit this website, fill in our forms, talk to us about the service, or use it as a client.

Called or texted one of our clients? If you called a business that uses Never Miss a Lead (for example a dog trainer), that business decides how your call, texts and voicemail are used. We handle them on its behalf as its data processor. Please see that business's privacy policy, or contact the business directly. If you contact us instead, we'll pass your request to the business straight away and tell you we've done so (see section 4).

3. Marketing

We may email you about our own similar services if you're a current or past client, or you asked us about the service, and you didn't opt out when you gave us your details. Otherwise we'll only send you marketing emails if you've agreed to them. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also tell us to stop at any time.

We may upload a scrambled (hashed) copy of email addresses to Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to show our adverts to people who've been in touch, or to stop showing them to existing clients. Meta matches and then deletes the scrambled data. You can object to this at any time and we'll remove you.

4. Our clients' callers

When someone calls, texts or WhatsApps a business that uses Never Miss a Lead, that business is the controller and we are its processor. We handle the caller's number, call times, voicemails, texts, WhatsApp messages and (on AI plans) AI call transcripts and summaries only on the business's instructions, under a written data processing agreement.

  • Voicemails are recorded, and the greeting says so.
  • On Smart Text and AI Receptionist, an AI assistant may reply to texts and WhatsApp messages using only information the business has given it, and passes anything else to the business.
  • On AI Receptionist, an AI receptionist may answer calls the business misses. It says it's an AI assistant and that the call is transcribed. The audio isn't recorded; a transcript and summary are kept.
  • Anyone can reply STOP to stop texts from that business.

If you're a caller and you contact us about your information, we'll pass your request to the business within [2 working days], tell you we've done so, and help the business respond.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it if it's wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete it, or to limit how we use it, in some circumstances;
  • object to how we use it where we rely on legitimate interests, and object at any time to marketing;
  • ask for information you gave us to be sent to you or another organisation in a reusable format, in some circumstances;
  • withdraw your consent, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email info@bigbark.media or WhatsApp us. We'll reply within one month. If your request is complex we may need up to two more months, and we'll tell you why. If we need more information to find what you've asked for or to confirm who you are, the time limit starts once we have it. There's normally no charge.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've used your information, please tell us first: see our complaints page. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice when our services or the law change, and show the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information in a significant way, we'll tell our clients by email first.
