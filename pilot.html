The pilot

What you get, and what we ask

  1. 50% off your first 3 monthly payments. Your half price starts the day your number goes live, not the day you sign up. After 3 months it's the normal monthly price, and you can cancel any time.
  2. We set everything up for you behind the scenes. You fill in a short form (about 5 minutes), we register your number, and you do three quick jobs when it's ready (about 15 minutes).
  3. In return, honest feedback on how the set-up went: a few questions by WhatsApp or email. No calls.

The checkout page will mention a free trial. As a pilot you skip the trial and go straight onto the half price, which starts when your number is live. Nothing is charged before then.
