How it works

What happens when you can't pick up

Calls still ring you first, exactly as they do now. Never Miss a Lead only steps in when you don't answer.

  1. Someone calls and you're busy

    You're on a session, on a walk, driving, or it's gone 7pm. The call rings out.

  2. Mobile callers get a text straight away

    In your business's name and your words, with your booking link if you have one. Anyone can also leave a voicemail.

    "Hi, it's Paws & Progress. Sorry we missed your call. How can we help?"

  3. You get an alert and reply when you're free

    By text and email: who called, what they said, and their voicemail. Reply from your dashboard or just text back from your phone.

Keeps your existing number. Unanswered calls on your mobile divert to us with a short code. We send you the code for your network and a short video to walk you through it. Nothing changes for your callers.

Built for dog trainers, walkers, groomers and behaviourists, and works the same for any UK service business that can't always get to the phone.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Do I need a new number?

No. You can keep the number everyone already has. We give you a UK number that sits behind the scenes, and your mobile sends calls you don't answer to it.

I run Facebook or Google ads. How does this help?

We recommend pointing your ads at WhatsApp (on Smart Text and AI Receptionist). On Facebook and Instagram, people tap your ad and a WhatsApp chat with your business opens. On Google, your website's WhatsApp button does the same. The AI replies in seconds, any time of day, and your dashboard shows which ad each enquiry came from, so you can see which ads are worth the money.

What happens to my existing number?

Nothing changes for callers. Your phone still rings as normal. You dial a short code (we send it to you, matched to your network) so that calls you don't answer, or calls when you're busy or out of signal, go to Never Miss a Lead instead of your voicemail. You can switch it off again with one code.

Most UK networks support this. Vodafone Pay As You Go doesn't, so we'll use a new number instead. Some tariffs charge for diverted calls, so check yours.

Does it work with my booking system?

Yes, alongside it. Add your booking link and it goes in the text, so callers can book straight away. It works with any booking system that has a link, including Calendly, Acuity, Fresha and your own website. It doesn't connect into your diary itself.

What will the AI text assistant say to my customers?

Only what you tell it. When we set you up, you give us the basics: your services, prices, the area you cover, when you run classes and how to book. It's instructed to use just that to answer questions, get the customer's details and send your booking link, and to hand anything else to you. Like any AI it can get things wrong, so check its replies in your dashboard, especially in your first few weeks.

If someone mentions a bite, aggression, a complaint or anything it isn't sure about, it says you'll be in touch and hands the chat straight to you. The moment you reply yourself, it goes quiet on that chat. You can pause it or switch it off any time in your dashboard.

What about GDPR?

It's built with UK GDPR in mind. You stay in control of your callers' data and Big Bark Media handles it on your behalf under a data processing agreement. Every text names your business and lets people reply STOP.

Voicemails are recorded, and the greeting says so. AI receptionist calls aren't recorded, but they're transcribed and summarised so you can call back, and the AI says so at the start of the call. Message contents, voicemails, AI transcripts and summaries are deleted after 12 months. We keep a basic log of calls (number, time, outcome) while you're a client, for billing and support.

We give you wording for your privacy policy. For how we handle your own details, see our privacy notice.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There's no minimum term. Cancel from "Manage billing" in your dashboard, or just message us. Cancel before your free trial ends and you won't be charged anything. After that it's month to month, and a cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you've paid for.

When you cancel we email you the codes to switch your diverts off (one code, ##002#, does them all). Do that before your plan ends. We also email you a copy of your data 30 days after it ends, then delete your callers' details.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

On Smart Text and AI Receptionist, yes. We set up a WhatsApp Business profile on your Never Miss a Lead number, so your own WhatsApp stays as it is. WhatsApp messages land in the same inbox as your texts, and the AI can reply to them too. Meta has to check your business first, which usually takes a few days.

What if I'm not on a smartphone?

That's fine. Alerts arrive as normal text messages and email. To reply to a caller from any mobile, just text our number with the short code from the alert, like "#4821 Yes, I have space on Thursday". The dashboard is there if you want it, but you don't need it.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few working days. Under Ofcom's UK phone number rules, our phone provider has to verify the business behind every number before it goes live, so we'll ask for some business details. If you're a sole trader, the provider also needs photo ID: we'll send you a secure link for it. While that's happening, we write your texts and set everything up behind the scenes. When your number's live, we email you your dashboard link and short setup videos: dial your divert code, turn on alerts and test it with a missed call. If you get stuck, email or WhatsApp us.

Your 14-day free trial doesn't start until your number is live, so the wait doesn't eat into it.
