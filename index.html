How it works

What happens when you can't pick up

Calls still ring you first, exactly as they do now. Never Miss a Lead only steps in when you don't answer.

  1. Someone calls and you're busy

    You're on a session, on a walk, driving, or it's gone 7pm. The call rings out.

  2. They get a text from you straight away

    From your business, in your words, with your booking link if you have one. They can also leave a voicemail.

    "Hi, it's Paws & Progress. Sorry we missed your call. How can we help?"

  3. You get an alert and reply when you're free

    By text and email: who called, what they said, and their voicemail. Reply from your dashboard or just text back from your phone.

Keeps your existing number. Unanswered calls on your mobile divert to us with a short code we'll walk you through, or we give you a new local number for your website and ads. Usually both.

Built for dog trainers, walkers, groomers and behaviourists, and works the same for any UK service business that can't always get to the phone.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Do I need a new number?

No. You can keep the number everyone already has. We give you a UK number that sits behind the scenes, and your mobile sends calls you don't answer to it. If you'd like, we can also give you a new local number to put on your website or ads, which rings your mobile first.

What happens to my existing number?

Nothing changes for callers. Your phone still rings as normal. You dial a short code (we send it to you, matched to your network) so that calls you don't answer, or calls when you're busy or out of signal, go to Never Miss a Lead instead of your voicemail. You can switch it off again with one code.

Most UK networks support this. Vodafone Pay As You Go doesn't, so we'll use a new number instead. Some tariffs charge for diverted calls, so check yours.

Does it work with my booking system?

Yes, alongside it. Add your booking link and it goes in the text, so callers can book straight away. It works with any booking system that has a link, including Calendly, Acuity, Fresha and your own website. It doesn't connect into your diary itself.

What will the AI text assistant say to my customers?

Only what you tell it. When we set you up, you give us the basics: your services, prices, the area you cover, when you run classes and how to book. It uses just that to answer questions, get the customer's details and send your booking link. It won't make up prices or promise anything you haven't told it.

If someone mentions a bite, aggression, a complaint or anything it isn't sure about, it says you'll be in touch and hands the chat straight to you. The moment you reply yourself, it goes quiet on that chat. You can pause it or switch it off any time in your dashboard.

Is it GDPR-compliant?

It's built for UK GDPR. You stay in control of your callers' data and Big Bark Media handles it on your behalf under a data processing agreement. Every text names your business and lets people reply STOP. Call records, messages and voicemails are deleted after 12 months. We give you a paragraph to add to your privacy policy, and the voicemail greeting tells callers they're being recorded.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There's no minimum term. Cancel during the 14-day trial and you won't be charged anything. After that it's month to month. If you cancel, any tracking number we gave you keeps ringing through to your phone so you have time to update your listings, and one code switches your mobile divert off.

What if I'm not on a smartphone?

That's fine. Alerts arrive as normal text messages and email. To reply to a caller from any mobile, just text our number with the short code from the alert, like "#4821 Yes, I have space on Thursday". The dashboard is there if you want it, but you don't need it.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few working days. Under Ofcom's UK phone number rules, our phone provider has to verify the business behind every number before it goes live, so we'll ask for some business details. Then we set up your texts with you and do a test call together.
