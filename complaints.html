Complaints

Last updated 18 October 2026 · Version 1.0

We want to put things right quickly. This page explains how to complain about Never Miss a Lead, or about how we've handled your personal information, and what happens next. It's free to complain.

How to complain

  • Email: info@bigbark.media, with "Complaint" (or "Data protection complaint") in the subject
  • WhatsApp: 07822 882162
  • Post: Complaints, Big Bark Media (Ogilvie Dogs Ltd), Isla Farm, 4 North Drove, Bourne, Lincolnshire, PE10 0BJ

Please tell us your name, how to contact you, your business name if you're a client, what went wrong, and what you'd like us to do. If someone is complaining for you, let us know. If you need us to do anything differently to help you make a complaint, just say.

Complaints about your personal information

This covers anything about how we collect, use, keep or share personal information, including a request you've made to see or delete your information.

StepWhat we doWhen
1. AcknowledgeWe confirm we've got your complaint, who's dealing with it, and when to expect an answer.Within [5 working days], and always within 30 days (the legal limit)

If you're still unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113. The ICO usually expects you to have complained to us first.

Called or texted one of our clients?

If your complaint is about a call, text, voicemail or AI reply from a business that uses Never Miss a Lead, that business is responsible for your information and should handle your complaint. Contact them directly. If you contact us, we'll pass your complaint to the business within [2 working days], tell you we've done so, and help them answer it.
